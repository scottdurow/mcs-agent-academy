# Repurpose Fabrikam content for social

From the provided long-form content, produce:

1. **LinkedIn** (~1,300 characters): a story-led hook, 3 short takeaways, a CTA.
1. **X/Twitter** (≤280 characters): one sharp line with one relevant hashtag.
1. **Instagram caption:** a punchy first line, then 3–5 hashtags at the end.

Keep each platform's voice native. Never just truncate the blog — rewrite for the channel.

## Example

Input: a 700-word blog post announcing the Trailburst shorts.

Output:

- **LinkedIn** opening "Pockets that bounce ruined my morning runs. So we fixed it." → three takeaways → "Grab yours Friday."
- **X:** "The zip pocket that finally stays put. Trailburst drops Friday. 🏃 #Running"
- **Instagram:** "Run light. Carry everything. 🏃‍♀️" + `#Trailburst #RunningShorts #FourWayStretch #FabrikamFitness`
